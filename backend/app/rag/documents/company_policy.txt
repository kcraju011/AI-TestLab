Company Leave Policy

Employees receive 20 annual paid leave days per calendar year.

Employees receive 10 sick leave days per calendar year.

Employees must submit annual leave requests at least 5 working days before the planned leave.

Sick leave requests should be submitted as soon as reasonably possible.

Unused annual leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days.

Employees should contact the Human Resources department for questions about leave approval.
